Physiological Maturity & Readiness Indicators

Visual Color Test

80% to 85% of grains or panicles should turn golden yellow/straw color. The upper leaves may start drying while lower stems remain slightly green.

Terminal Water Cutoff

Drain standing water 10 to 14 days prior to harvest. This facilitates field drying, firms soil for harvesters, and hardens grains uniformly.

Moisture Content Check

Ideal grain harvest moisture is 20-25%. If harvested too dry (<18%), grain shattering losses increase; if too wet (>25%), drying costs spike.

3-Day Weather Window

Check the Weather Advisory page to guarantee a minimum 48-72 hour dry weather window before initiating manual cutting or combine harvesting.
